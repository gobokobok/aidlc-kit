About this site

A read-only view of work done with AIDLC Kit.

What the kit does

Product Managers take an AI feature from discovery and requirements through acceptance criteria, the AI prompt, evaluation and an engineering handoff, from their IDE and in plain language, with no Git branches, commits or merge requests to handle. Underneath, every item is versioned and owned, the right people approve it, the AI is tested against agreed cases, and the history is kept.
